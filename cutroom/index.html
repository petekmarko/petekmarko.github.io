03 / Node grading

LUTs, grades and colour spaces. In a node tree.

The picture flows left to right through each node. Convert the camera's colour space, drop in your .cube LUTs, add a grade, then fix just the water with a secondary. Switch nodes off to compare, and save the whole tree as a preset.
