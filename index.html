About me

Hello, I'm Marko Petek

I’m Marko Petek, a Slovenian professional kayaker with a background in competitive slalom and a passion for creeking. I’ve paddled at World and European Championships as part of the Slovenian national team and have explored whitewater across New Zealand, Norway, Italy, Austria, the Zambezi, and beyond. With a degree in Sports Science and years of experience on the water, I’m always looking for the next river and the next challenge.
